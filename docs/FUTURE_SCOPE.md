# Possible future scope

The following ideas are intentionally not implemented in the MVP. They are candidates for future validation, not roadmap promises.

- Creator discovery database or marketplace
- Email finder or automated creator outreach
- Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube API integrations
- Automatic post metrics or reporting
- Native payouts, Stripe Connect, or bank integrations
- Invoice generation
- Contract generation or e-signatures
- AI creator recommendations or AI-generated outreach
- Creator-facing portal
- Content upload, approval, or creative-draft comments
- Client-facing reports or white-label workspaces
- Multiple workspaces, team invitations, roles, and permissions
- Complex campaign analytics
- Native mobile application
- Browser extension
- WhatsApp or SMS notifications
- Annual billing or per-creator pricing

Any future feature should be validated against the core principle: Fluebaze must remain faster and less intimidating than a spreadsheet for small D2C teams.
